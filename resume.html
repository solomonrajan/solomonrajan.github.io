Executive Summary & Professional Philosophy

Core Profile

I am a seasoned Human Resources Professional & Labour Welfare Officer whose career began in specialized clinical and medical social work. Over the past five years, my professional trajectory has evolved dynamically—advancing through positions as Medical Social Worker, Child Welfare Officer, Personnel Executive & Welfare Officer, and currently, Officer, Labour Relations at Madura Coats.

My foundational philosophy centers on fostering a cooperative, dignified workplace culture while driving bulletproof statutory compliance and operational excellence. I specialize in the complete lifecycle of industrial relations: from collective bargaining support, dispute resolution, and grievance arbitration, to end-to-end payroll administration and statutory inspections.

In parallel with human capital leadership, I possess a deep affinity for modern technology and software architecture. As an open-source Android developer and web technologist, I leverage software engineering principles to streamline HRMS platforms, automate data pipelines, and design privacy-first tools that enhance organizational efficiency.
