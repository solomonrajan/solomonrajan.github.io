Hello! I am Solomon Rajan, a seasoned Human Resources Professional whose journey began as a Medical Social Worker. Over the years, my career has evolved dynamically, progressing through roles such as Child Welfare Officer, Labour Welfare Officer, Personnel Executive-HR, and currently, Officer Labour Relations.

My philosophy centers on fostering a positive workplace culture and ensuring operational excellence. I specialize in comprehensive HR activities, including end-to-end Payroll administration, strict adherence to Statutory compliances, and holistic Employee and Office management.

I am highly proficient in leveraging modern technology to streamline HR operations, with extensive experience in implementing and managing advanced HRMS platforms and the latest HR-related software solutions to optimize organizational efficiency.
